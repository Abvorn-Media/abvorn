# Instagram Content Skill — Abvorn

## Platform Rules
- **Tone:** Aspirational, warm, community-focused
- **Max Hashtags:** 30
- **Caption Length:** 200–500 chars
- **Hook Style:** Visual anchor, curiosity, aspiration
- **Visual Style:** Polished, aesthetic, warm tones

## Viral Hook Templates
- "The {product} that changed my mind"
- "I didn't believe the hype... until I tried this"
- "Here's what {product} actually looks like in real life"

## Carousel Format
1. Hook / promise (slide 1)
2. The problem
3. The verdict / score
4. The breakdown
5. The comparison
6. CTA to full review

## Anti-Patterns
- × No aggressive CTAs
- × No overly salesy language
- × No more than 30 hashtags
- × No low-quality images
