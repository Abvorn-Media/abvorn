# LinkedIn Content Skill — Abvorn

## Platform Rules
- **Tone:** Professional, authoritative, insightful
- **Max Hashtags:** 5
- **Caption Length:** 500–1000 chars
- **Hook Style:** Insight bomb, data-driven, professional
- **Visual Style:** Clean, professional, data-heavy

## Viral Hook Templates
- "Here's what 50 hours of testing {product} taught me"
- "The {product} market is $X billion. Here's who's winning."
- "I analyzed 10,000 reviews of {product}. The results surprised me."

## Anti-Patterns
- × No casual/generic takes
- × No more than 5 hashtags
- × No engagement-bait (#Like #Follow)
- × No unsubstantiated claims

## Winning Format
1. Data-backed hook
2. Structured insight (small paragraphs or bullets)
3. Verdict with score
4. CTA to the full review
