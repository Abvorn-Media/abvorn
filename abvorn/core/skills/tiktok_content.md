# TikTok Content Skill — Abvorn

## Platform Rules
- **Tone:** High-energy, punchy, trend-aware
- **Max Hashtags:** 5
- **Caption Length:** 100–200 chars
- **Hook Style:** Pattern interrupt, controversy, curiosity
- **Visual Style:** Bright, fast-paced, text overlay

## Viral Hook Templates (Oliver Henry Formula)
- "I tested {product} for 30 days... here's the truth" 🔥
- "Stop buying {product} until you see this" 🔥
- "My {person} said I was wasting money... until they tried this" 🔥

## Anti-Patterns
- × No long paragraphs
- × No complex language
- × No more than 5 hashtags
- × No boring hooks

## Winning Format
1. Hook on slide 1 (text overlay)
2. Problem on slide 2
3. Verdict on slide 3
4. Breakdown on slide 4
5. Comparison on slide 5
6. CTA on slide 6
