Buying Guide: How to Choose the Right Webcam in 2026

Solving the "which webcam should I buy" problem requires answering a few key questions about your specific situation:

1. Do you need 1080p or is 720p sufficient?

For most video calls in 2026, 720p is acceptable, especially on smaller laptop screens or mobile devices. However, if you're regularly presenting on large monitors or TVs, sharing recorded content, or want a future-proof setup, 1080p is worth the modest price premium. The jump from 1080p to 4K webcams is rarely worth it for non-streamers.

2. How important is audio quality?

If you're currently using your laptop's built-in microphone, any external webcam with a dedicated mic will sound dramatically better. If you're in a noisy household or take calls in public spaces, prioritize dual-microphone setups like the EMEET C960. For podcast-quality audio, however, no webcam microphone will suffice; you'll need a dedicated USB microphone.

3. What's your lighting situation?

If you can't control your lighting (working near windows, under harsh fluorescents, etc.), auto-light correction features become critical. Logitech's implementation across the Brio 101, C270, and C920x is particularly effective
